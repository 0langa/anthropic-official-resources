# Engineers, agents, and Black Friday: what Shopify learned at peak

Every Black Friday, millions of merchants run their biggest weekend on Shopify. This year, AI agents shop alongside their customers. Shopify’s engineers spent nine months getting the platform ready, with help from their own agents that review deploys and suggest load tests. Farhan Thawar, VP and Head of Engineering, shares what the weekend showed about Shopify’s systems, people, and agents.
