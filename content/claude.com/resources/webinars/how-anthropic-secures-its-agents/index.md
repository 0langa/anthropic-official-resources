# How Anthropic secures its agents

Giving an agent real access means planning for three things: overreach, hijacking and misuse. Human approval alone won't catch all of them, so Anthropic starts from a harder assumption: any single layer can fail.

Join John Lopus and Liam Plambeck for a look at how we put that assumption into practice, in our products and in our own engineering, where AI authors 80% of merged code. We'll walk through the three layers we stack: the model and its safeguards, the controls you run, and monitoring. Then we'll turn it into concrete steps you can apply to your own agents.

## What you’ll learn

- Contain the blast radius: use sandboxes, egress allowlists, and single-purpose agent identities to limit what an agent can do, whatever it's told.
- Review at agent speed: tier code by risk, run narrow and independent review agents, and save human review for what matters most.
- Earn trust in automation: pilot new reviewers in shadow mode, red-team them, and sample their approvals.
- See what your agents do: send every agent action to your SIEM and treat unusual behavior as insider risk.
