# Building your first workflow with Cowork

Join Anthropic's Customer Success Team for a step-by-step guide to launching your first workflow with Cowork. Ideal for brand new users, this session will give you an overview of what Cowork is, how to get started, and how to get the most value out of it as your agentic partner. In 45 minutes, we'll walk you through a live build-along — building a personal weekly digest from scratch — followed by Q&A, so you walk away with practical next steps for using Claude as part of your daily workflow.

This session will be most valuable if you come with connectors ready to connect!

## What you’ll learn

- Cowork basics - connectors, skills, and plugins explained simply, and when to use Cowork vs. Chat.
- Building your weekly digest - a live, hands-on build of a personal digest that pulls together your priorities, calendar, and updates for the week.
- Permissions, demystified - what Cowork is asking for when it requests a permission, and what to look for before approving.
- Making it stick - turning your digest into a scheduled task so it runs automatically every week, plus where to find what's enabled for your org.
