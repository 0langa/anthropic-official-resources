## What you’ll learn

- Set up identity and access: connect SSO and SCIM, then use groups and custom roles to give each team the right access and tools.
- Choose your org defaults: default model and model access, data retention, and the settings that matter most for solid governance.
- Extend Claude’s abilities: which connectors, skills, and plugins to enable first, and what to configure in the Claude app, Cowork and Claude Code.
- Avoid common mistakes: the setup issues admins hit most often and how to prevent them.
