# Claude for Nonprofits: An Admin’s Guide to Data Handling and AI Policy

For the admin in charge of your nonprofit’s Claude settings: answer “how does Claude handle our data?” in writing, put a one-page AI use policy in place, and invite your staff with confidence.

Many teams hold off inviting staff until someone writes down how Claude handles their data and what staff may use it for. This session does both, as a live walkthrough of the Claude Academy tutorial, Claude for Nonprofits: Data handling and your AI use policy. At every step we say whether it applies to Team, Enterprise or both.
