## What you’ll learn

- How to set your files up for success — so Claude understands your templates, slide masters, and data structures from the start, and generates outputs that fit seamlessly into your existing workflow rather than requiring heavy cleanup.
- How to prompt with intent — moving from basic requests to techniques that let you target specific slides or cells, chain edits without losing context, and get outputs that actually match your organization's standards and voice.
- How to work across apps — Claude can now open, edit, and pass context between Excel and PowerPoint, so you can move a chart directly into a presentation without the usual copy-paste friction. We'll show you how to use this capability as part of a end-to-end workflow.
- How to connect Claude to the tools you already use — with connectors for Google Drive, Gmail, and dozens of other enterprise tools, you can bring live data and context directly into your decks and spreadsheets without switching between apps.
