# Claude for Nonprofits: A Staff Guide to Making Claude Useful on Day One

Most nonprofit staff on Claude use it the same way: open a chat, paste something in, get a decent but generic answer, and go back to doing it by hand. It isn’t you. The work that matters lives in your files, your donor records and your colleagues’ heads, and Claude can’t see any of it yet.

This 45-minute session for staff is built to be useful the same day. Bring the piece that’s due (for most people right now, the year-end appeal). On a made-up nonprofit’s data, and with you doing the first step alongside us, Claude learns where your things live and how your organization writes. You see what changes when the tool your work lives in is connected, and the Claude for Nonprofits skills already in your account take that piece from plan to a draft in your voice. You leave with one piece to send this week, one thing to do again next week, what your AI use policy lets you put in, and a short note for whoever manages your account. Team and Enterprise both covered and labeled.

- How the Claude for Nonprofits skills already in your account take the piece that's due — a GivingTuesday appeal in the session — from plan to a checked draft in your organization's voice, and the same pattern for a grant report or a board update.
