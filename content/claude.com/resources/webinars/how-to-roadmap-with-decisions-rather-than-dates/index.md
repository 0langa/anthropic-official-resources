# How to Roadmap with Decisions Rather Than Dates

Many of the "best practices" of engineering leadership have been built around managing dates, timelines and priorities. However, as AI harnesses have improved over the past two years, it's increasingly possible to lead in a new way: running a roadmap that is constrained by decisions rather than dates. In this session, we'll discuss how to move towards operating in a decision-constrained engineering organization.
