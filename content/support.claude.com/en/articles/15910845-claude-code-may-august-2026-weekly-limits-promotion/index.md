# Claude Code May–August 2026 weekly limits promotion

This promotion was a limited-time boost on top of your plan. It ran from May 13 through September 13, 2026. We were planning to return weekly limits to their original levels, but we know many of you found the extra usage helpful. So while the full promotional boost couldn't last, we're making part of it permanent: starting September 14, 2026, weekly limits in Claude Code are 25% higher than they were before the promotion for Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans. We have a lot more in the works around usage, visibility, and control, so stay tuned.

We offered a limited-time promotion that increased weekly usage limits in Claude Code by 50%.

This promotion was available for Pro, Max, and Team plans, as well as legacy seat-based users on Enterprise plans. Free plans and consumption-based Enterprise seats were not included in this promotion.

## What was the promotion?

From May 13, 2026 through September 13, 2026, your weekly usage limit in Claude Code was 50% higher. 5-hour usage limits were not affected by this promotion.

## Eligibility

No action was required to participate. If you were on an eligible plan, the increased weekly limit was automatically applied to your account.

## Where did this apply?

The 50% increase applied to Claude Code only, everywhere you use it: the CLI, IDE extensions, desktop, and the web. Usage limits for other Claude products, like Claude (web, desktop, and mobile) and Claude Cowork, were unchanged.

## Frequently asked questions

### Did I need to do anything to get the extra usage?

No. The promotion applied automatically. You saw the higher weekly limit reflected in your Claude Code usage without any changes to your account settings. You were able to run `/usage` in the CLI to view your updated limits.

### Did this change my 5-hour usage limit?

No. This promotion increased weekly usage limits only.

### I'm on an Enterprise plan. Was I included?

Legacy seat-based users on Enterprise plans were included. Consumption-based Enterprise seats were not included in this promotion.

## Terms and conditions

This offer was valid from May 13, 2026 through September 13, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT. It applied to Pro, Max, and Team plans and to legacy seat-based users on Enterprise plans only, and excluded Free plans and consumption-based Enterprise seats. This offer had no cash value and was not transferable. It could not be combined with other offers.
