## Where the relationship stands

$994,550 billed against $985,000 budgeted across 11 open matters — on plan overall (+1.0%). Three matters drive all of the overage: Meridian v. Calloway (+$73,650), the Series C financing (+$23,400) and the Dawson claim (+$7,900). Twenty-six entries worth $48,310 break a guideline; four more are set aside below for you to check first.

## Points to raise

1**Meridian v. Calloway is 35% over budget with the expert phase still ahead.** The damages expert retained in May ($61,200) was outside the engagement-letter scope and was not raised with us first, which §6.1 requires for any disbursement over $10,000. **Suggested ask:** a revised budget through trial by 15 July and written approval before further expert spend.  
*Evidence: Guidelines §6.1 · Inv 24-11267 line 41 (expert retainer) · matter-budgets-FY26.xlsx row 2*

2**Block-billed entries above the four-hour cap.** Eleven entries this quarter combine several tasks in one narrative over four hours, which §3.2 of our guidelines asks the firm to itemize; together they come to $21,460, most of it on Meridian v. Calloway in June. **Suggested ask:** re-issue the June invoice with those entries itemized, or apply the 15% reduction §3.2 allows.  
*Evidence: Guidelines §3.2 · Inv 24-11284 lines 7, 12, 19, 23 · Inv 24-11301 lines 3–5 · Inv 24-11276 lines 9, 14, 21, 30 — full list in HL-Q2-spend-review.xlsx, “Off-pattern entries”*

3**Research billed at partner rates.** Six research entries (12.5 hrs, $12,950) were billed by partners where §4.1 expects associate staffing unless we agree otherwise; 6.2 hrs sit on the Series C matter. **Suggested ask:** re-rate to the blended associate rate.  
*Evidence: Guidelines §4.1 · Inv 24-11284 line 19 · Inv 24-11290 lines 4, 11, 15 · Inv 24-11301 lines 8, 12*

4–5**More than two timekeepers at one meeting or deposition** (§4.4; 4 entries, $8,600) and **clerical tasks billed by fee-earners** (§3.5; 5 entries, $5,300) — listed in the appendix; raise together as a staffing note.

**Check before raising**

Four entries may be fine and I could not tell from the invoice alone: two “attend team meeting” entries by J. Whitcombe (1.5 and 1.2 hrs) may fall under the §4.4 exception for strategy meetings the client requested; one 5.8-hr research entry cites a memo I did not find in the matter file; one paralegal entry for “coordinate court reporter” may be billable under §3.5(b). Worth a look at the invoices before the call.

### Why this works[](https://academy.claude.com/use-cases/outside-counsel-review)

Prompt

**Check against your guidelines, not a generic rule.** "Off-pattern against our billing guidelines" means the flag is your block-billing or staffing rule, not a textbook one.

Prompt

**Ask for the variance and the driver.** A matter over budget is a number; why it's over is the conversation, so each row names the activity that pushed it.

Prompt

**End with the deliverable.** "Draft the talking points" turns the analysis into the prep you'd write anyway, so the partner conversation starts from substance.

Source

**Pull from the matter system directly.** The iManage or NetDocuments connector means the review covers every matter and invoice, not the ones that made it into a spreadsheet.

Practice

**Ask it to flag uncertainty.** Add "flag anything you're not confident about" so you know which entries to verify against the invoice before you raise them.
