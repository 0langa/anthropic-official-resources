**Where v4.2 stands.** Leads on 5 of 13 heuristics and trails on 8; furthest behind on H1 visibility of system status (2.1 against Northstar 3.6). Mean score: us 2.7, Northstar 2.7, Pallet 3.0, Kitewire 2.4.

we trail by 1.0 or more we trail by less competitor’s own low score · scale 0–4, gap = us − best competitor

## Patterns to adopt

1**A setup progress rail that stays on screen and says how long is left.** Northstar keeps a five-step rail pinned to the left through the whole flow with “about 4 minutes left” under it; you always know where you are and what remains, which is where our flow scores lowest. Our v4.2 shows a step count only on steps 2 and 3 and nothing on the data-connection step, the longest one.Evidence `northstar-onboarding-03-invite-team.png`, `-04-connect-data.png`, `-05-progress.png` · H1 Northstar 3.6 vs us 2.1 · C1 3.4 vs 2.0

2**Undo on every setup step, and “finish later” that resurfaces on the home screen.** Pallet lets people back out of an import or a template choice without losing the rest, and parks anything skipped as a card on Home.Evidence `pallet-onboarding-04-import.png`, `-07-home.png` · H3 Pallet 3.5 vs us 2.5

3**A keyboard path through setup for expert users.** Kitewire shows shortcut hints on step 5 and every field advances on Enter; the whole flow completes without a mouse.Evidence `kitewire-onboarding-05-shortcuts.png` · H7 Kitewire 3.4 vs us 2.2

## Patterns to avoid

1**A workspace URL that cannot be changed later, with no confirmation.** Northstar commits the subdomain on blur; two testers in their own forum threads report re-creating workspaces.Evidence `northstar-onboarding-02-workspace-url.png` · H5 Northstar 1.8

2**An icon-only role picker with no labels or descriptions.** Kitewire’s three role glyphs carry no text until hover and no accessible name.Evidence `kitewire-onboarding-04-roles.png` · H6 Kitewire 2.1 · C3 1.8

3**Help that opens external docs in a new tab and loses the setup state.** Pallet’s “?” leaves the flow; returning reloads step 1.Evidence `pallet-onboarding-06-help.png` · H10 Pallet 2.2
