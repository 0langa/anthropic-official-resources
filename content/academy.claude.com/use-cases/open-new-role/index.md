# Senior Product Manager, Growth

<table class="props"><tbody><tr><td><span>Company</span>Fernway</td><td><span>Level</span>P5</td><td><span>Reports to</span>Head of Growth</td><td><span>Location</span>London (hybrid) or remote UK</td><td><span>Req</span>GR-014</td></tr></tbody></table>

## Why this role, why now

Self-serve signups grew 41% year on year while activation (a workspace with three active members inside 14 days) stayed at 23%. The Growth squad, four engineers and one designer, has had no dedicated PM since June.

## What this person will own

- Activation and onboarding
- Referral program
- Self-serve to sales-assist handoff (accounts crossing 20 seats)

**Not in scope:** pricing and packaging tests (Monetisation), lifecycle messaging (Marketing).

## Must-have

1. Has owned an activation or onboarding metric end to end for a year or more. **Tested in:** metric deep-dive.
2. Has run an experimentation program from hypothesis to readout at a self-serve product. **Tested in:** review of one of their past experiments.
3. Writes SQL well enough to answer their own funnel questions. **Tested in:** 30-minute live query on our sample schema.
4. Presents a roadmap to executives and defends the trade-offs. **Tested in:** roadmap presentation. *From intake: replaces “strong communicator”.*

## Nice-to-have

- Pricing or packaging exposure.
- Has managed a PM or an associate.

## Level

P5 per leveling-guide-2026.pdf §3: "owns a product metric and its roadmap end to end; influences one adjacent team." Not P6: no cross-team direction setting expected in year one.

## What good looks like at 90 days

<table class="d90"><tbody><tr><td><i></i></td><td>Three activation experiments running at once with a weekly readout the squad writes</td></tr><tr><td><i></i></td><td>First shipped change to onboarding</td></tr><tr><td><i></i></td><td>Presents the 2027 growth roadmap at the January leadership review</td></tr></tbody></table>

## Interview loop

1. Recruiter screen
2. Hiring manager
3. Metric deep-dive (Data lead)
4. Experiment review (Eng lead)
5. Roadmap presentation (Head of Growth, CPO)
6. Values

## Open for the recruiter

1. **Comp band**P5 London band pending Finance sign-off; hiring manager to confirm by 2 Sep.
2. **Remote outside the UK**Manager open to EU time zones; needs a People team policy check.
3. **Referrals timing**Whether referrals move to this role on day one or after the Q4 campaign.
4. **Earliest start**Against the January roadmap review the day-90 outcome depends on.

Each must-have is written as something an interviewer can test.

### Why this works[](https://academy.claude.com/use-cases/open-new-role)

Prompt

**Ask to be interviewed before it drafts.** "Walk me through the intake"
makes the conversation the deliverable, so the spec is built from your
answers rather than guessed from a job title.

Prompt

**Give permission to push back.** "Push back where I'm vague" gives Cowork
permission to do what a good recruiter does: notice when "strong
communicator" needs to become a testable signal.

Prompt

**List open questions separately.** Asking for "what's still open for the
recruiter" keeps undecided comp bands or location flex out of the spec and
on a short list for the live sync.

Source

**Put your templates in the working folder.** Your spec template and
leveling guide sit in the working folder, so "our standard format" and the
level definition mean the same thing for every manager who runs this.

### Get a better draft[](https://academy.claude.com/use-cases/open-new-role)

Practice

**Add an example to match.** Drop an example you like into the folder and
Cowork matches your structure and voice.

Practice

**Ask it to flag uncertainty.** Add "flag anything you're not confident about"
so you know where to look first when you review the draft.
