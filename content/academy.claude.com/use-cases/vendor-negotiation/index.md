## Our leverage

- 208 of 250 seats were active in the last 90 days (42 idle, $49,900 a year at their price).
- Two comparable quotes on file: Contoso $86 and Fabrikam $91 per seat, both with premium support included.
- Two SSO outages in Q2 and a service credit that took six weeks to apply.
- We are a referenceable logo in logistics and would sign 36 months for the right price.
