### Why this works[](https://academy.claude.com/use-cases/competitive-teardown)

Prompt

**Name the competitors, not the category.** Two or three named products keeps the research targeted and the matrix readable; "the market" turns into a survey nobody finishes.

Source

**List the sources to browse.** "Product, docs, changelog, pricing page, and recent reviews" sets the research scope so the teardown covers what they ship, what they charge, and what customers actually say.

Source

**Use internal sources, not your website.** "Our docs and Figma" means your column in the matrix reflects what you've actually built and designed, not what marketing says on the website.

Prompt

**Ask what still needs a response.** "Everything they shipped last quarter that we haven't answered" turns the changelog into a backlog conversation, separate from the evergreen positioning.
