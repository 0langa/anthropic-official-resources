Operating income was $1,018K, $18K ahead of budget and $68K below February. ## Commentary by line

**Cloud hosting: $596K, $66K (12%) over budget and $84K (16%) above February. Unfavourable, continuing.** The events-ingest pipeline went live on 10 March and roughly doubled compute hours on the ingest cluster for the rest of the month; the ledger detail shows the AWS invoice at $548K against $466K in February, with CDN and monitoring flat at $48K. The new load runs at on-demand rates because reserved-instance coverage fell from 78% to 61% when it came online. April will carry a full month of it, about $95K over budget, unless the reserved-capacity purchase infrastructure has proposed is approved; that decision is on the agenda for the 9 April review.

**Professional services: $268K, $67K (20%) under budget and $42K (14%) below February. Unfavourable, reverses.** Two implementations moved go-live to April at the customers’ request; $58K of milestone billing moves with them and contract values are unchanged.

**Marketing programs: $176K, $62K (26%) under budget and $29K (14%) below February. Favorable, timing.** The spring campaign launch moved from 24 March to 14 April; $55K of agency and media spend is committed for April and the full-year plan is unchanged.

**Subscription revenue: $4,265K, $55K (1%) over budget and $85K (2%) above February. Favorable, recurring.** Expansions at two enterprise accounts added $48K and nine new customers net of churn added $37K, which lifts the Q2 starting run-rate.

**Contractors: $98K, $42K (30%) under budget and $48K (33%) below February. Favorable, continuing but smaller than it looks.** Two data-engineering contractors became employees on 1 March; about $31K of their cost now sits in salaries, which still finished $38K under budget on two open roles, so the net monthly saving is nearer $17K than $48K.

**Software and subscriptions: $131K, $39K (42%) over budget and $43K (49%) above February. Unfavourable, likely one-off.** All of the movement is a single $36K invoice from the observability vendor posted on 18 March; every other subscription is within $2K of February. The invoice memo reads “annual platform renewal”, which would normally be booked to prepaids and expensed at $3K a month. To confirm: if accounting agrees this is an annual prepayment, March software spend is overstated by about $33K and the line is on budget. I could not tell from the ledger entry alone, so please check with accounting before the review.

## Lines under threshold

Salaries, support, travel, rent, professional fees and depreciation were each within 10% and $50K of both February and budget; together they were $31K under budget.

## To confirm before the review

1. Software invoice treatment (above).
2. April go-live dates for the two services customers, with the services lead.
