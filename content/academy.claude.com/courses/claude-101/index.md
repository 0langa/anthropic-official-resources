What you’ll learnBy the end of this course, you’ll be able to

- Explain what Claude is and have an effective first conversation with it
- Write clear prompts, refine them when results miss, and apply the AI Fluency framework to get better outcomes
- Distinguish the three shapes of work in the Claude desktop app — working turn by turn, handing work off, and building software — and recognize which one a task calls for
- Use Projects, Artifacts, and Skills to organize your work and give Claude reusable context
- Connect your tools, company knowledge, and the web with Connectors, Enterprise Search, and Research
- Apply Claude to tasks across common roles and explore Claude Code, Claude Tag, Claude Design, Claude for Microsoft 365, and Claude in Chrome

Claude 101 is the starting point for anyone bringing Claude into their everyday work. You'll go from your first conversation and the basics of effective prompting through to Projects, Artifacts, and Skills, then on to Connectors, Enterprise Search, and Research for working with your tools, your company's knowledge, and the web. Along the way you'll see how Chat, Cowork, and Code in the desktop app fit different kinds of tasks, and you'll finish with role-based use cases and a tour of Claude Code, Claude Tag, Claude Design, Claude for Microsoft 365, and Claude in Chrome.

## **Prerequisites**[](https://academy.claude.com/courses/claude-101)

- A Claude account on any plan (Free, Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise)
- No prior experience with Claude or other AI assistants needed
- A few features covered here (Cowork, Enterprise Search) require a paid or Team/Enterprise plan to try hands-on. Each lesson notes where this applies.

## **Who this course is for**[](https://academy.claude.com/courses/claude-101)

Anyone getting started with Claude at work. New users who want a guided path from first message to confident daily use, and existing users who want a structured tour of Projects, Artifacts, Skills, Connectors, Enterprise Search, and Research.
