From super suit to shared room

Most of us met AI as a chatbot assistant. We chatted one-on-one to create work products. It's a single-player experience.

Using single-player AI is like putting on an “AI super suit” at work. It helps you get more done on the individual tasks you're assigned.

When everyone on your team is using single-player AI, information can get siloed. Four people ask for similar work and learnings are not shared across the agents.

This is inefficient and everyone is operating off of slightly different information. This is parallel work, not team work.

Multiplayer AI is different. It's like being on the best team you've ever been on. Humans and agents operate in a shared space together.

For example, Mara asks once and Claude answers once, then everyone benefits and gets to read the same response, refining it together.

What the team writes in the room becomes institutional knowledge that the team and the company can build on.

Multiplayer AI accelerates alignment, and every piece of work an agent completes benefits the entire team's expertise.

## Key takeaways[](https://academy.claude.com/courses/building-effective-human-agent-teams/why-multiplayer-ai-matters)

- **Single-player AI speeds up an individual, but it can also silo information from the team.** Multiplayer AI ensures that humans and agents work side by side with shared context.
- **The faster a team grows,** the greater the impact siloed context can have on agent performance.
- **When agents and humans work together in a shared space,** agents are able to be more proactive and personalized.
