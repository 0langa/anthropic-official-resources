Welcome to AI Fluency for students

SummaryTranscript

This video introduces the concept of AI Fluency—using AI effectively,
efficiently, ethically, and safely, as well as what you'll learn in our *AI
Fluency for Students* course. Rather than focusing on temporary tips or
specific tools, the course explores enduring principles that help you make
thoughtful decisions about when and how to use AI. The video outlines the
course structure: starting with the 4D Framework, then exploring AI as a
learning partner and career planning tool, and finally discussing what it
means to be "the human in the loop." The goal is to transform AI from a tool
that does work for you into a partner that helps you do your work better.

Hello and welcome to AI Fluency for Students. I'm Zoe. I'm Maggie Vo. I'm Alex Albert.

In this course, we'll explore a different way of thinking about AI as a student, so that your interactions with AI can be effective, efficient, ethical, and safe. Most guidance about AI focuses on use cases and tricks that grow outdated with new models and products. But over the past few years, we've observed some skills that persevere. Those skills are what we call AI fluency. It goes beyond the models, beyond the tools, beyond the prompts, and asks important questions about what it means to engage well with AI in our lives and in our work.

How do we decide what to pass to AI entirely? How do we decide when not to use AI at all? How do we pick the right AI assistant and assess for accuracy and quality? Do we tell our teachers or employers when we've used AI? And if so, how? In this course, we'll explore all of these questions and more.

We'll start with an overview of the 4D framework: delegation, description, discernment, and diligence. Four competencies that, when combined, create AI fluency. Then we'll explore two practical applications of AI as a student. First, you'll learn how to bring AI into your learning in order to understand concepts more deeply. Second, you'll learn how to use AI in your career planning and job search to brainstorm future career paths, develop skills, and land a great role. Finally, we'll talk about what it means to be the human in the loop.

AI is becoming a part of almost every aspect of our lives, from how we learn and work to how we communicate and engage with the world around us. The leaders of tomorrow are those who can work thoughtfully and responsibly with AI systems in a way that keeps them at the center. By the end of this course, you'll have the confidence and skills to work with AI in ways that align with your values, support your growth, and prepare you for a future where AI fluency is essential. So let's dive in.
