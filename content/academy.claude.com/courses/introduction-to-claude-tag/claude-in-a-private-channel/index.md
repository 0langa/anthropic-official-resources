Some of your work includes information only specific people should see, like people matters, finance, legal work, or a customer escalation with confidential detail. You already keep that in a private channel, and Claude keeps the same boundary.

## What you can do in a private channel[](https://academy.claude.com/courses/introduction-to-claude-tag/claude-in-a-private-channel)

If you want that same team help on work only a specific group should see, ask Claude in that group's private channel.

- **The same channel work, for members only:** everything [lesson 3(opens in new tab)](https://academy.claude.com/courses/introduction-to-claude-tag/claude-in-a-public-channel) describes works here for the channel's members: threads anyone in the group can correct or continue, this channel's history and connected tools, untagged replies and routines.
- **Tools connected for members only:** a tool can be connected to this channel alone, the same way tools are connected to a public channel. In a private finance channel, for example, your billing system may be connected: only the channel's members can have Claude use it, and Claude can do only what the account created for it is allowed to do, which is kept to [the least access the work needs(opens in new tab)](https://claude.com/docs/claude-tag/admins/add-connections).
- **What the workspace has already taught Claude:** the channel benefits from the workspace notes Claude has saved in public channels, without adding to them.

## Protecting your privacy and data[](https://academy.claude.com/courses/introduction-to-claude-tag/claude-in-a-private-channel)

### The channel's boundary is Claude's boundary[](https://academy.claude.com/courses/introduction-to-claude-tag/claude-in-a-private-channel)

A private channel's boundary applies to Claude the way it applies to people.

- **Only members can have Claude read or act here:** from any other channel or DM, Claude behaves [as if this channel, its tools, and its memory do not exist(opens in new tab)](https://claude.com/docs/claude-tag/concepts/security-and-data), and its replies here are visible only to the channel's members.
- **What Claude learns here is saved to this channel only:** Claude saves it to this channel's notes and [never to workspace notes(opens in new tab)](https://claude.com/docs/claude-tag/users/memory), so confidential context cannot surface in someone else's channel.
- **Tools connected here are for members only, with the access they were given:** whatever that account can read is available to every member through Claude, so the member list is the access list. Keep it tight.
- **Every action is attributable:** Claude acts under its own account in the team's connected tools, so the tool's own log shows what Claude did and when. [How agent identity works(opens in new tab)](https://claude.com/docs/claude-tag/concepts/agent-identity) has the detail.
- **Guests and shared channels are handled by your admin:** whether Claude answers in a channel with guests, or one shared with another company, is an admin setting ([guest channels(opens in new tab)](https://claude.com/docs/claude-tag/admins/restrict-access)). Where it does answer, Claude keeps to that channel and does not search the rest of the workspace from there.

## Try it[](https://academy.claude.com/courses/introduction-to-claude-tag/claude-in-a-private-channel)

### Run one sensitive task in its private channel

In a private channel you are in · 10 minutes

1. Pick a recurring task that belongs in a private channel, and add Claude there with `/invite @Claude` if it is not already a member.
2. Ask `@Claude what can you access from this channel?`
3. Run the task.

**Done when:** the reply is visible only in that channel and uses only what that channel holds.

**Key takeaway:** a private channel's boundary is Claude's boundary; the same work, kept to its members.

**After practicing:** You will have run one sensitive task in a private channel, visible only to its members.
