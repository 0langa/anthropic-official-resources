# Getting Started with Claude Design

You don't need to be a designer to build a polished, on-brand deck, one-pager or prototype. If you have Claude Enterprise, you already have Claude Design. Most teams just don't know when to use it, or they haven't done the one setup step that makes every output follow their company's brand guidelines. This session covers both, so you can ace your next deliverable.

## What you’ll learn

- When to use Claude Design. Where it beats a general chat tool for decks, one-pagers, social graphics, and mockups, and where it doesn't.
- How to import your design system. The one setup step that keeps every deck, doc, and graphic on brand, with no designer in the loop.
- How to go from brief to final export. A live workflow from outline to PowerPoint, Google Slides, or your tool of choice.
