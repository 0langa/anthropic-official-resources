2. The prompt

Copy this into Claude

Audit my Q3 renewal book. For every account renewing in the next 90 days, score renewal risk red/yellow/green based on product-usage trend, recent sentiment, and open exec commitments. Explain the signal that drove each score, and publish it as an artifact I can share with leadership.

Renewals / FY26-Q3

Why this works

Prompt

Name the inputs to weigh. Usage trend, last sentiment, open commitments are the inputs that actually predict churn; saying so keeps the score explainable.

Prompt

Ask for the reason behind each score. Asking for the reason behind each score so each red includes why it's at risk — something you can act on.

Prompt

Say how you'll share the output. An artifact means leadership opens one link, not last Tuesday's spreadsheet.

Source

Pull from the authoritative source. The renewal date, ARR, and owner come from Salesforce, so the audit and the forecast agree.

Get a better draft

Practice

Add your scoring rubric. Drop your team's red/yellow/green definitions in the project and the scores match how your forecast call already talks.

Practice

Ask for next actions. Add "for every red, suggest the next action and who runs it" so the audit is also the save plan.

4. Make it repeatable

Share it as an artifact

A spreadsheet is a snapshot. With the audit published as an artifact, leadership opens one link. Ask Claude to add filters for segment, owner, and risk.

Run it on a schedule

Risk moves week to week. Ask Claude to set up a scheduled task, or open Scheduled in the sidebar, and /renewal-risk rescores the book and refreshes the artifact before your Monday forecast call.

Set up a scheduled task for every Monday at 7am: rerun /renewal-risk on the current 90-day book, refresh the artifact, and mark any account that moved to red since last week.

5. Share with your teammates

Your /renewal-risk skill now carries your usage thresholds and your save plays. Share it so every CSM and AE in the segment runs the same audit and the forecast call argues about action, not definitions.



Share the skill

You can share a skill you created. In your skills(opens in new tab), open the ... menu next to /renewal-risk, select Share, and pick your teammates. For your whole organization, open the skill and select Publish to org, if your admin allows. They get the skill with your instructions baked in, so they don't have to build it again.

What changes for the renewal motion

Every account in your renewal window has a risk score, the signal behind it, and an owner, in one shared view. You work the at-risk list instead of building it.

You did this for one renewal cycle. The same approach covers expansion reviews, forecast risk, and QBR prep — each one a skill your team runs the same way every time.
