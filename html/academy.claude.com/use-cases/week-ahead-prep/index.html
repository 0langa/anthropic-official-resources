The prompt

Copy this into Claude

Look at my calendar for next week. For each meeting, pull the context: the last time we met, related Slack threads, attached or mentioned docs, and open items I owe them. Draft a one-paragraph agenda for each and flag the three I most need to prep for. Write it all to a single week-ahead doc for Sunday night.

Planning / Week-of-2026-04-27

Why this works

Source

List the sources to pull from. Listing "last time we met, related Slack threads, attached docs, open items I owe" tells Claude exactly which sources to chase per meeting, so the brief is grounded rather than generic.

Prompt

Ask which ones matter most. "Flag the three I most need to prep for" makes Claude commit to a priority call instead of treating every meeting as equal weight.

Prompt

Ask for one combined output. "Write it all to a single week-ahead doc" gives you something you can read top to bottom on Sunday instead of clicking through a folder of files.

Source

Send output to a folder you'll reuse. The Week-of folder is where the briefing, the agendas, and your own notes accumulate through the week, so Friday's retro can read straight from it.

Get a better draft

Practice

Add an example to match. Drop an example you like into the folder and Claude matches your structure and voice.

Practice

Ask it to flag uncertainty. Add "flag anything you're not confident about" so you know where to look first when you review the draft.

Make it repeatable

Run it every Sunday at 5pm

The briefing should be waiting before the week starts. Ask Claude to set up a scheduled task, or open Scheduled in the sidebar, and /week-ahead runs every Sunday afternoon and writes to a fresh Week-of folder.

Share with your teammates

Your /week-ahead skill now carries your skip list, your prep tiers, and your agenda format. Share it so your directs and your EA produce briefings in the same shape, and anyone covering for you walks into Monday with the same context you would.



Share the skill

You can share a skill you created. In your skills(opens in new tab), open the ... menu next to /week-ahead, select Share, and pick your teammates. For your whole organization, open the skill and select Publish to org, if your admin allows. They get the skill with your agenda format and prep tiers baked in, so they don't have to build it again.
