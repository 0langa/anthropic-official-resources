Meeting prep and a weekly recap in your DM with Claude

In a direct message, set up a routine and Claude Tag posts a prep note before each of your meetings from your own calendar, email and documents, and drafts a Friday recap you can forward.

10 minOperationsClaude Tag

In a DM with Claude Tag, Claude reads your own calendar, email and documents, so it can brief you before each meeting and draft your recap on Friday.

Set up a routine(opens in new tab) in the DM by telling Claude once when to post and what to include. Claude then posts a prep note each morning for that day's meetings. Each Friday, it drafts the week's recap as a note(opens in new tab) you can forward. Only you can read the DM. Claude sends nothing for you, and changes your calendar only when you ask.

Tips

Direct messages need your own Claude account

Direct messages with Claude use a Claude account of your own in the organization, and an Owner can turn them off for everyone. If Claude does not answer in a DM, ask your admin.

Use a channel of your own when someone else should see the posts

The same routines can run in a channel with only you and Claude in it, so people you invite can read the posts (work from your own channel(opens in new tab)). To use your own calendar, email or documents there, Claude asks your permission, accesses them as you, and lets you preview what it will post before it appears in the channel.
