Getting started with Claude for nonprofits

Learn how to access Claude's nonprofit pricing, connect to fundraising and donor management tools, and get your team started.

3 minClaude
Open Claude

Claude for nonprofits enhances mission-driven organizations with specialized capabilities, connecting to donor management platforms, foundation research databases, and collaboration tools while maintaining transparency and accountability. Learn more on the nonprofit solutions page(opens in new tab).

How to access nonprofit tools and features

Claude for nonprofits is available on the Team and Enterprise plans at nonprofit pricing. See current prices on the nonprofit solutions page(opens in new tab).

Both plans include access to the same models as our standard Team and Enterprise plans, with a minimum of 2 seats for teams under 150.

To get started, verify your nonprofit status through our partner Goodstack by completing this short form(opens in new tab). The verification process takes 2–3 minutes for Team plans. For Enterprise pricing, contact our sales team(opens in new tab).

Nonprofit operations and fundraising connectors

Claude connects to nonprofit platforms through the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Nonprofit connectors include:

  • Benevity(opens in new tab): Access a database of validated nonprofits for cause discovery and research
  • Blackbaud(opens in new tab): Connect to Raiser's Edge NXT for donor records, gift history, events, and personalized communications
  • Candid(opens in new tab): Search nonprofits and foundations with detailed profiles, financials, and grant data

Additional connectors include Microsoft 365, Gmail, Google Drive, Asana, Slack, and HubSpot. Browse all available connectors in the connector directory(opens in new tab).

Frequently asked questions

Who is eligible?

501(c)(3) organizations and their international equivalents, K–12 schools, and qualifying healthcare organizations.

Who is not eligible?

Government agencies, political campaigns, higher education institutions, and healthcare systems.

How does validation work?

We partner with Goodstack(opens in new tab) for nonprofit verification. Team plan validation takes 2–3 minutes. If you have issues with verification, contact Goodstack(opens in new tab).

How long does the discount last?

The nonprofit discount remains active as long as your organization maintains its nonprofit status.

What if I already have a full-priced account?

Go to Admin settings > Billing(opens in new tab) to update your plan, or reach out to our support team(opens in new tab).

Is my data used for training?

Note: By default, Anthropic does not use data from Team or Enterprise accounts to train models. Learn more at our Privacy Center(opens in new tab) and Trust Center(opens in new tab).

Can I get a tax exemption?

Submit your tax exemption certificate through support(opens in new tab) to receive a refund and apply the exemption to future invoices.

What about organizations with fewer than five employees?

The Team plan at nonprofit pricing has a minimum of 2 seats, so small organizations can use it too. Individual users can access Claude Pro at $20/month. Explore the AI fluency for nonprofits course(opens in new tab) and the use case library(opens in new tab) to get started.

What support and training is available?

Visit the use case library(opens in new tab) and help center(opens in new tab) for resources and guidance.

Troubleshooting

  • Discount link not working: Clear your browser cache and try again in an incognito window. If the issue persists, contact support(opens in new tab).
  • Team plan option greyed out: Make sure you're signed in to the correct account and have admin permissions
  • Email domain questions: All team members don't need the same domain. Learn about allowed email domains(opens in new tab).
  • Connecting tools: Learn how to browse and connect tools from the directory(opens in new tab).
